# WELL PROGRAM & DRILLING CASING DESIGN: WELL 104-B
Document ID: WP-NS-104B
Well Name: Well 104-B
Basin / Field: North Sea Alvheim Block 24/6
Total Planned Depth: 12,500 ft MD

1.0 GEOLOGICAL STRATIGRAPHY & LITHOLOGY
The wellbore trajectory traverses the following stratigraphic sequence:
- Brent Sandstone: Top depth 8,400 ft MD. Highly porous deltaic sandstone interval with excellent reservoir permeability.
- Draupne Formation: Top depth 9,850 ft MD. Organic-rich reactive shale with severe sloughing tendencies and potential pack-off hazard.
- Eagle Ford Shale (analog reference section): Sensitive to mud water loss and high differential sticking potential.

2.0 BOTTOM HOLE ASSEMBLY (BHA) & EQUIPMENT ASSETS
- Drilling Assembly: 8-1/2 inch PDC Bit with Positive Displacement Mud Motor
  - Mud Motor Serial Number: MM-775-A1
  - MWD Directional Sensor: MWD-TENSOR-882
  - Casing String: CSG-958-L80 (9-5/8 inch casing at 8,200 ft shoe)

3.0 DRILLING RISKS & MITIGATION PROCEDURES
Due to unstable shales in Draupne and high borehole tortuosity, the primary operational hazard is drillstring pack-off (FAIL-PACKOFF) and mechanical stuck pipe.
All drillers and directional contractors must adhere to SOP-SP-774 (Drillstring Packoff Remediation) whenever standpipe pressure fluctuates by more than 250 psi.
